50 AI Automations You Can Build This Weekend — The Complete List
These are not ideas. These are automations that real companies deployed.
Save this :)
Most AI content tells you what is theoretically possible. This list tells you what is actually happening.
50 automations organized by department. Each one includes what it does, how it works, and the real impact businesses reported after deploying it.
If you run a business, this is your menu. Pick the ones that match your pain points and build them.

Sales (01 to 10)

01. Inbound Lead Qualifier
AI agent reads new form submissions, researches each company online, scores against ICP criteria, and routes qualified leads to the right rep. Businesses report 60 to 70 percent reduction in time-to-first-contact because reps only see pre-qualified leads.
02. Cold Email Personalizer
Agent takes a prospect list, researches each company's recent news and activity, and generates personalized first-line openers for cold outreach. Reported 2x to 3x improvement in reply rates versus template emails.
03. CRM Data Enrichment
Agent monitors CRM for new contacts with missing fields, researches each one, and fills in company size, industry, tech stack, funding status, and social profiles. Saves sales ops teams 5 to 10 hours per week of manual research.
04. Meeting Prep Brief
Before every sales call, agent generates a one-page brief: company background, recent news, previous interactions, likely objections, and suggested talking points. Reps walk into calls prepared in 30 seconds instead of 15 minutes.
05. Proposal Auto-Drafter
Rep enters basic project details. Agent generates a complete proposal using the company's template, pricing structure, and standard terms. Average proposal creation time dropped from 2 hours to 15 minutes.
06. Win/Loss Analyzer
Agent analyzes closed deals (won and lost), identifies patterns in deal size, sales cycle length, competitors involved, and objection types. Produces monthly analysis that surfaces why deals are won or lost.
07. Follow-Up Sequencer
After meetings, agent drafts follow-up emails referencing specific discussion points, attaches relevant case studies, and schedules send times. Eliminates "I forgot to follow up" entirely.
08. Competitive Objection Handler
When a rep logs that a prospect mentioned a competitor, agent pulls the latest competitive intelligence and generates specific counter-messaging for that competitor's weaknesses.
09. Pipeline Health Monitor
Agent reviews the pipeline daily. Flags deals that have stalled, identifies deals with missing next steps, and alerts managers to pipeline risks before they become problems.
10. Quote Calculator
Agent takes project requirements, applies pricing rules, calculates discounts based on deal size, and generates formatted quotes. Eliminates pricing errors and reduces quoting time from hours to minutes.

Marketing (11 to 20)

11. Content Repurposer
Takes one long-form piece and produces a Twitter thread, LinkedIn post, newsletter section, Instagram caption, and three standalone social posts. One piece of content becomes seven or more pieces across platforms.
12. SEO Brief Generator
Agent researches target keyword, analyzes top-ranking content, identifies content gaps, and generates a detailed writing brief with recommended structure, word count, subtopics, and internal linking opportunities.
13. Social Listening Summarizer
Monitors brand mentions across social platforms. Generates daily digest: positive mentions, negative mentions, trending themes, and suggested responses for anything requiring attention.
14. Email Campaign Analyzer
After each email campaign, agent pulls performance data, compares against benchmarks, identifies what worked and what did not, and generates recommendations for the next campaign.
15. Blog Post First Drafter
Takes an approved brief and voice guide, produces a complete first draft matching brand voice. Marketing writers report spending 60 percent less time on first drafts and more time on strategic editing.
16. Ad Copy Variant Generator
Takes a core message and generates 10 to 20 ad copy variants for A/B testing across platforms. Includes headline variations, description variations, and CTA variations.
17. Competitive Content Tracker
Monitors competitor blogs, social accounts, and email newsletters. Produces weekly report on what competitors published, what topics they are covering, and content gaps they are leaving open.
18. Event Follow-Up Automator
After conferences or webinars, agent processes attendee lists, scores engagement level, drafts personalized follow-up emails for each tier, and queues them for sending.
19. Customer Testimonial Processor
Agent monitors reviews, support ratings, and NPS responses. Identifies positive sentiments suitable for testimonials, drafts polished versions, and organizes them by use case for the marketing team.
20. Landing Page Copy Optimizer
Agent analyzes existing landing page performance data, identifies high-bounce sections, and generates alternative copy for underperforming elements with A/B test recommendations.

Customer Support (21 to 28)

21. Tier-1 Auto-Responder
Agent handles common support questions using product documentation as its knowledge base. Resolves 40 to 60 percent of incoming tickets without human involvement. Escalates complex issues with full context to human agents.
22. Ticket Classifier and Router
Every incoming ticket is automatically categorized by type, severity, and product area, then routed to the correct specialist. Eliminates manual triage and reduces average response time.
23. Knowledge Base Gap Finder
Agent analyzes support tickets weekly to identify questions that customers ask repeatedly but the knowledge base does not answer. Generates draft articles to fill each gap.
24. Customer Sentiment Tracker
Processes all customer interactions (tickets, reviews, survey responses) and generates weekly sentiment reports. Tracks changes over time and flags emerging negative trends early.
25. Escalation SummarizerWhen a ticket escalates to a senior agent, the AI generates a complete summary: original issue, troubleshooting steps already taken, customer sentiment, and recommended resolution path. Senior agents start with full context instead of reading 20-message threads.
26. Proactive Issue Detector
Agent monitors error logs and system metrics. When patterns suggest a potential customer-facing issue, it drafts a proactive communication to affected customers before they contact support.
27. Response Quality Auditor
Samples 10 percent of human agent responses daily. Evaluates against quality standards. accuracy, tone, completeness, resolution. Generates coaching feedback for managers.
28. Self-Service Deflection Optimizer
Analyzes which support issues could be resolved by self-service but are not. Recommends specific improvements to help docs, FAQ pages, and in-app guidance to reduce ticket volume.

Operations (29 to 38)

29. Invoice Processor
Reads incoming invoices (PDF, email, image), extracts vendor, amount, line items, payment terms, and due date. Enters data into accounting system and flags discrepancies.
30. Expense Report Builder
Processes receipt images from a folder. Extracts vendor, amount, date, and category. Generates formatted expense reports with category totals and policy compliance checks.
31. Meeting Notes Processor
Takes meeting recordings or transcripts. Produces structured summaries with action items, decisions made, open questions, and owner assignments. Distributes automatically to attendees.
32. Document Classifier
Incoming documents (contracts, proposals, reports, invoices) are automatically classified by type, named consistently, and filed to the correct folder. Eliminates manual file management.
33. Onboarding Checklist Manager
When a new employee is added to the system, agent generates a personalized onboarding checklist, schedules orientation tasks, sends welcome emails, and tracks completion.
34. Vendor Comparison Analyzer
When evaluating vendors, agent researches each option, compiles pricing, features, reviews, and references into a standardized comparison document. Cuts vendor evaluation time by 70 percent.
35. Contract Clause Scanner
Reads contracts and highlights non-standard clauses, missing protections, unusual terms, and potential risks. Flags specific sections for legal review with explanations of why they need attention.
36. Weekly KPI Dashboard Builder
Agent pulls data from multiple sources weekly, calculates key metrics, compares against targets and previous periods, and generates a formatted dashboard with trend analysis.
37. Inventory Alert System
Monitors stock levels. When any item drops below threshold, agent generates reorder recommendations based on lead times, consumption rates, and supplier pricing.
38. Process Documentation Generator
Agent observes a workflow (through task logs and descriptions), documents each step, creates a standard operating procedure, and identifies steps that could be further automated.

HR and Recruiting (39 to 45)

39. Resume Screener
Reads incoming resumes, matches against job requirements, scores candidates on relevant experience, skills, and qualifications. Produces a ranked shortlist with reasoning for each score.
40. Job Description Writer
HR inputs role requirements. Agent generates a complete job posting matching company voice and style, optimized for the target platform (LinkedIn, job board, company site).
41. Interview Question Generator
Based on the job description and candidate resume, agent generates tailored interview questions that probe specific experience claims, assess cultural fit, and evaluate technical competencies.
42. Performance Review Summarizer
Takes manager notes and self-evaluations. Generates structured performance review documents with strengths, development areas, and suggested goals for the next period.
43. Employee FAQ Bot
Answers common employee questions about policies, benefits, PTO, expenses, and procedures using the company handbook as its knowledge base. Handles 60 to 80 percent of HR inquiries without human involvement.
44. Exit Interview Analyzer
Processes exit interview transcripts across all departures. Identifies recurring themes, risk factors for attrition, and specific actionable recommendations for retention.
45. Compensation Benchmarker
When setting salaries for new roles, agent researches market data, adjusts for location and experience level, and generates a compensation recommendation with supporting evidence.

Finance (46 to 50)

46. Cash Flow Forecaster
Agent pulls historical financial data, accounts receivable and payable, and recurring expenses. Generates 30/60/90-day cash flow forecasts with scenario analysis.
47. Budget Variance Analyzer
Compares actual spending against budget monthly. Identifies categories with significant variances, explains likely causes, and recommends adjustments.
48. Financial Report Narrator
Takes raw financial statements and generates a plain-English narrative explaining what happened, why it matters, and what to watch for next period. Board-ready in minutes instead of hours.
49. Tax Document Organizer
Processes a year of receipts, invoices, and financial documents. Categorizes by tax-relevant category, flags missing documentation, and generates a summary ready for the accountant.
50. Subscription Auditor
Scans company expenses for recurring subscriptions. Identifies unused or underutilized subscriptions, calculates total spend, and recommends cancellations with projected savings.

How to Use This List

Do not try to build all 50. Pick the three that would save your business the most time and start there.
Sort by pain. Which tasks consume the most employee hours in your organization? Which ones do people complain about most? Those are your starting points.
Start with one per department. Let one automation prove its value before expanding. A successful automation generates internal buy-in for the next one.
Measure everything. Track time saved, error rates, and cost per operation for every automation. The data makes the case for continued investment.
That is it. 50 automations. All real. All running. All measured.
